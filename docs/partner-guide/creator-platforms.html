Bring shoppable video to your creators

Your creators upload a video, tag the products in it and publish it on their own site. Their audience watches and buys without leaving the video.

How it works

1

You share one partner code, or a sign-up link with it filled in. Each creator gets 30 days free, with no card.

2

The creator uploads a video, tags a brand's products as they appear, and publishes the player on their own site.

3

Viewers buy inside the video. Each account that joins with your code is credited to you, and you see its sales.

5. Putting the player on a page LIVE

Every published video has a Copy embed code button, so a creator never writes these by hand. They are here for your team, for pages you build yourselves.

PartMeaning
{videoId}The video's ID on MTRLZD.
{trackingCode}Credits the views, product clicks and sales on that visit to one page or campaign. Without it, the video's own default code is used.

Shoppable player link

https://www.mtrlzd.com/embed/{videoId}?utm={trackingCode}

Try a live example: www.mtrlzd.com/embed/830068a4-1a47-4a1e-85ff-3f8215939c08

Web embed

<iframe src="https://www.mtrlzd.com/embed/{videoId}?utm={trackingCode}"
  width="100%" height="450" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen
  allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture"></iframe>

Script widget

The video with a row of its products under it, drawn into the page by one script tag.

<div id="vc-widget-{videoId}"></div>
<script src="https://www.mtrlzd.com/embed/{videoId}/widget.js?utm={trackingCode}" async></script>

Playlist embed

A curated set of shoppable videos on one page.

<div data-playlist="{playlistId}"></div>
<script src="https://www.mtrlzd.com/embed/playlist.js" async></script>

MTRLZDNext · Partner API

The "Connect MTRLZD" plugin NEXT

The Partner API lets a creator connect their MTRLZD account inside your platform once, then see their shoppable videos, copy a player link and read their results without leaving your product. This is the planned shape, shared so your team can review it early. It is not live yet, and we'll finalise it with your feedback.

Connecting an account

Your creator taps "Connect MTRLZD", signs in and approves the connection. Your platform receives a key for that account and the creator can revoke it at any time. Account keys already exist in MTRLZD's settings and will be the credential the API accepts.

Planned endpoints

RequestReturns
GET /api/partner/v1/videosThe account's published videos: title, cover image, player link.
